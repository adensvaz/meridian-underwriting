08 · Analyst narrative

68ch measure · 3px accent rule · provenance in micro

Marisol Residences underwrites to AED 688,900 of stabilised net operating income against a purchase price of AED 12,480,000 — a 5.52% net yield and a 7.43% gross, both inside the JVC band for 2019-vintage freehold stock. The pricing of AED 1,346 per sq ft sits 4.2% under the submarket median, and the discount is almost entirely explained by unit 303, vacant since 14/03/2025.

Leverage is the constraint rather than the yield. At 60% LTV the deal clears its 1.20× covenant with 0.12× of headroom, but cash-on-cash falls to 3.18% against a 4.00% mandate floor. Re-letting 303 at the AED 83,000 market rent recovers roughly 70 bp of that gap without any assumption about rental growth.

Two structural items carry the residual risk: only eleven of twelve tenancies are Ejari-registered, and four of the twelve are on one- or two-cheque terms, which concentrates collection risk into the first quarter of each contract year.
